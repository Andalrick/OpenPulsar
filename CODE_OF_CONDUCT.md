# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in OpenPulsar a respectful and harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level or personal characteristics.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful, constructive and technically honest.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and explain decisions when practical.
- Respect privacy and do not publish private data or unrelated USB captures.
- Accept maintainers' decisions about scope, safety and release readiness.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, intimidation, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate disruption, doxxing and publication of private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainer through the contact method listed on the maintainer's GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

This code of conduct applies in all project spaces and when representing the project publicly.
